Who You Are: new location field (sandbox mockup)

Three options for the Location question. A: a small map. "Use my location" asks the browser for your position and drops the pin; drag the pin or tap the map to move it; the city shows under the map. B: a zip or city box (suggestions as you type) with a small "Use my location" link. C: a compact row ("Tap to set on map"); tapping it opens the map as an overlay sheet like the city picklist, with search, Use my location, a draggable pin and DONE. Nothing is saved (sandbox). Open one alone with wya_grokbot.html?opt=A&demo=1 (or opt=B / opt=C).
